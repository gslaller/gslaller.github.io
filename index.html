Experiment 2026-08-28

Temporal Locality in Sparse Expert Routing

A baseline study of how often nearby tokens reuse the same experts in a sparse mixture-of-experts model, and whether that locality is stable across layers, prompts, and decoding regimes.

Research note 2026-08-14

Memory Tokens as a Compression Boundary

What happens when later tokens can no longer attend to the original context and must rely on a small learned summary? This note sketches the setup, the information bottleneck, and a few measurements that make the experiment informative.

Visualization 2026-07-31

A Small Atlas of Hidden-State Geometry

Collecting hidden states across prompts, reducing them with PCA and UMAP, and looking for structure that survives changes in prompt wording, generation length, and model scale.

Essay 2026-07-12

Why Small Experiments Still Matter

Large models are useful, but they are often bad microscopes. A short argument for building reduced experiments that isolate one mechanism at a time before spending compute on scale.

Potpourri 2026-06-26

Notes on Sampling, Control, and Latent Directions
